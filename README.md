One funny one-line review a dog gives the mailman, under 200 characters — a one-star review with a three-year grudge and a punchline at the end. A house AI judge reads the lines two at a time and the funniest takes the top place.

> One star: he shoves paper through my door, rattles my gate, then runs — and after three years he still won't stay for the only tip I have: my teeth.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
